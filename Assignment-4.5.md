Assignment 4.5 — Multi-Page College Club Website
What to build: A complete college club website with four pages.
Required pages:
- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — Club introduction
- `registration.html` — Club registration form
- `contact.html` — Contact details
Steps:

1. Build consistent navigation across all pages.
2. Show club introduction and activities on the home page.
3. Provide club details and member information on the about page.
4. Build a student registration form on the registration page.
5. Show email, location, and contact information on the contact page.
6. Add an information table, such as an events schedule.
7. Add metadata and a favicon.
8. Test all internal links.
